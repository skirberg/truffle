# Code of conduct

Truffle is for anyone curious about what land is worth. Anyone taking part here, in issues and pull requests, can ask a basic question and get a kind answer.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- **Do:** be kind, assume good faith, explain rather than correct, and accept feedback on your work gracefully.
- **Don't:** harass anyone, make personal or political attacks, or publish anyone's private information. That includes posting someone's home, address or property details to single them out.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) option on the comment, issue or pull request. Reports reach the maintainer privately and are handled in confidence.
